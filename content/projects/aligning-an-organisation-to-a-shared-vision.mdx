## Overview

As the UX Design Lead at Hargreaves Lansdown, I led a cross-functional initiative to define and communicate a unifying organisational vision. The goal was to support company-wide digital transformation and improve alignment across 2,000 employees.

### ****Challenge

<Challenge>

### To transform fragmented departmental goals into a cohesive, compelling five-year vision that would inspire action and inject powerful significance into day-to-day work across the organisation.

</Challenge>

Despite being a well-established financial services provider, Hargreaves Lansdown had become siloed across marketing, digital, and operational departments. The lack of a shared vision and disjointed communications were stalling progress and dampening morale.

The CTO tasked us with creating a compelling, motivational vision to be launched at a major internal conference.

## Approach

### Framing the problem

Although I had experience crafting product visions, an organisational vision was new territory. I began with exploratory research into:

- The importance of organisational purpose and goals.
- Storytelling principles from Disney and Pixar.
- Vision frameworks (e.g. Product Vision, Visiontype, Provotypes).
- Internal organisational storytelling (e.g. Mailchimp’s internal brand video).

### Engaging the organisation

To create a vision grounded in reality, we needed input from all corners of the business:

- I planned and facilitated workshops with product, operations, marketing, compliance, and digital teams, uncovering recurring themes, goals, and frustrations.
- I supported the Product Lead in running stakeholder****interviews with senior leaders, revealing departmental strategies and roadmaps.

We synthesised the data to uncover shared values and future aspirations.

### Developing the narrative

Using insights gathered, I drafted multiple narrative arcs using classic story structure:

- What challenge we face
- What change we seek
- What future we’re building

I presented draft storyboards to the C-suite for feedback and aligned the story with HL’s broader transformation goals.

### Prototyping the vision

Before committing to engaging with the agency to start animation:

- I created a low-fidelity prototype—a narrated PowerPoint mock-up of the storyboard.

This allowed fast iteration and early stakeholder buy-in.

### Production and iteration

- We partnered with CX Partners to produce a **simple animated explainer**, balancing storytelling ambition with budget and timeline constraints.
- I worked closely with the agency and the CTO on daily reviews, ensuring creative execution aligned with vision and tone.

## Outcome

<Video provider="vimeo" id="1111575834" title="Visiontype" />

The vision video premiered at a company-wide conference to an audience of 2,000 employees and received spontaneous applause.

But more importantly, it:

- Improved cross-team collaboration by aligning goals under a common purpose.
- Increased motivation and engagement, especially in digital and operational teams.
- Laid the strategic foundation for HL’s digital transformation roadmap.

---

## Key learnings

This project reinforced just how powerful storytelling can be as a leadership tool. Crafting a clear, emotionally resonant narrative helped unite colleagues across departments and gave them something to believe in - something bigger than their day-to-day roles.

I also learned that one of the most valuable contributions a design leader can make is creating space for others to be heard, then synthesising those diverse perspectives into something coherent and inspiring.

And while I’m used to prototyping in product design, this experience reminded me that prototyping ideas (like a vision) can be just as effective. By treating the narrative as a living artefact and iterating rapidly, we were able to build alignment, confidence, and momentum.
