The principles guiding how I work

### Build trust

</Principle>

<Principle>

### Prioritise purpose

I challenge myself and others to stay focused. Making sure every workshop, prototype, and decision has a clear goal and delivers value.

</Principle>

I bring clarity and optimism in uncertainty, creating diagrams and models to help people move forward with confidence.

</Principle>

<Principle>

### Create space

I guide rather than control, creating the conditions, tools, and frameworks for others to take ownership and deliver their best work.

</Principle>

</Principle>
