These six principles guide how I build teams and lead design.

<Columns>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Build trust

I lead with openness and honesty to create the safety for people to share, experiment, and challenge with confidence.

</Principle>

</Column>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Prioritise purpose

I challenge myself and others to stay focused. Every workshop, prototype, and decision must have a clear goal and deliver value.

</Principle>

</Column>

</Columns>

<Columns>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Clarify complexity

I bring clarity and optimism in uncertainty, creating diagrams and models to help people move forward with confidence.

</Principle>

</Column>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Create space

I guide rather than control, creating the conditions, tools, and frameworks for others to take ownership and deliver their best work.

</Principle>

</Column>

</Columns>

<Columns>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Amplify others

I measure my personal success by the success of my team. When I give feedback or direction, they know it comes from a place of trust and care.

</Principle>

</Column>

<Column>

<Principle>

### Craft culture

I believe good design comes from healthy, collaborative teams, so I co-create the rituals and artefacts that keeps values alive in practice.

</Principle>

</Column>

</Columns>
